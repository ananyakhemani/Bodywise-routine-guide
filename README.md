# Routine Guide for Be Body Wise

A small web app that helps customers use five Be Body Wise products (face wash, shampoo, underarm roll-on, body wash, body lotion) correctly, and gives them an honest timeline for results.

Built for the Mosaic Fellowship Builder Challenge, Part 2.

## The problem
Customers like the products but are left alone after buying: how much to use, what to pair it with, what is safe after shaving, and when to expect results. Results are slow and mixed up with season and other products, so customers cannot tell if the product is working.

## What it does
- Asks 3 short questions about the product chosen
- Gives a personal plan: how to use, pair with, avoid, watch out for
- Shows a results timeline with the stage the user is in
- Lets the user check in and gives safety guidance
- Warns when an improvement coincided with a season change or a new product
